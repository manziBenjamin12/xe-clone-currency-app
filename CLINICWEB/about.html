ℹ️ About Rural Care Clinic Centre

Version 1.0.0

Complete Offline Patient Management System

Designed specifically for rural clinics with limited or no internet connectivity. All data is stored locally on your device.

📋 Features

  • 📊 Dashboard with real-time statistics
  • 📝 Patient Check-in (new/returning patients)
  • 👥 Complete Patient Registry
  • 📅 Appointment Scheduling
  • 📈 Reports & Analytics
  • ⚙️ System Settings
  • 💾 Backup & Restore functionality
  • 🔒 100% offline - No internet required
  • 📀 Data stored locally in browser

💡 How to Use

  • 1. Go to Check-in page to register new patients
  • 2. View all patients on the Patients page
  • 3. Schedule appointments on Appointments page
  • 4. Export your data regularly on BackUp Data page
  • 5. All data saves automatically to this device
